📚 How Card Counting Works

🐠 What is Hi-Lo Card Counting?

Hi-Lo is the world's most popular card counting system. You assign a value to every card you see, keep a running total called the Running Count, and use it to know when the remaining deck is rich in high cards — which is good for the player.

When the count is high, you bet more. When it's low, you bet the minimum. This is the bet spread, and it's how counters gain an edge over the casino over time.

🔢 The Hi-Lo Card Values

Assign each visible card a tag as it's dealt:

2 = +1
3 = +1
4 = +1
5 = +1
6 = +1
7 = 0
8 = 0
9 = 0
10 = -1
J = -1
Q = -1
K = -1
A = -1

Low cards (2–6) leaving the deck are bad for you (they helped the dealer bust less) → count as +1 when gone. High cards (10s, Aces) are good for you → count as -1 when gone.

📊 Running Count vs. True Count

Running Count (RC): Simply add your tags as each card is dealt. Start at 0 at the beginning of a new shoe.

True Count (TC): Divide the Running Count by the number of decks remaining in the shoe:

True Count = Running Count ÷ Decks Remaining

The True Count normalizes for how far into the shoe you are. A RC of +10 with 5 decks left is a much weaker signal than +10 with 1 deck left. Always base your bets on the True Count in multi-deck games.

💰 Bet Spread Strategy

Use the True Count to decide how much to bet. A rough guide for a 6-deck game:

True CountPlayer EdgeRecommended Bet
TC ≤ 0~−0.5%Table Minimum (1 unit)
TC = +1~0%1 unit
TC = +2~+0.5%2–4 units
TC = +3~+1.0%6–8 units
TC = +4~+1.5%10–12 units
TC ≥ +5~+2.0%+Max bet (up to 20 units)

Each +1 to the True Count adds ~0.5% to your edge. Use the Bet Spread sliders above to set your unit range.

🎯 Deck Penetration

Casinos shuffle early to kill your edge — this is called shallow penetration. You want deep penetration (as many cards as possible dealt before the shuffle).

  • Good: 75%+ of the 6-deck shoe dealt before reshuffle
  • Acceptable: 65–75%
  • Bad: Less than 60% — avoid this table

⚠️ Camouflage & Casino Heat

Casinos don't like card counters. Signs you're getting heat:

  • Pit boss standing close watching your hands closely
  • Mid-shoe shuffles right when you raise your bet
  • Being asked to "flat bet" (same bet every hand)
  • Being "backed off" — asked not to play blackjack

Blend in: vary your raises slowly, don't jump from minimum to maximum instantly, occasionally make a "mistake" bet. The most powerful — but also most obvious — technique is wonging (counting from outside, then sitting down only when the count is high).

🐟 The Big Picture

Card counting isn't a get-rich-quick scheme. It gives you a small, consistent edge over thousands of hands. Key numbers to understand:

  • A skilled Hi-Lo counter averages about 0.5–1.5% edge over the house
  • Variance is very high — big losing sessions are normal even with an edge
  • Need a bankroll of 200–300 max bets to safely survive the swings
  • Speed matters: more hands per hour = more of that edge realized

🎴 How to Play Baccarat
Closest to 9 wins. Cards 2–9 are face value, 10/J/Q/K = 0, Ace = 1. Only the last digit counts (e.g. 7+8=15 → 5). Each side gets two cards; a third card may be drawn by fixed rules (a natural 8 or 9 stands). Player pays 1:1. Banker pays 0.95:1 (5% commission). Tie pays 8:1, and Player/Banker bets are returned on a tie.
